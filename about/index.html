Who we are

Santee Pro Plumbing is a plumbing service based at 9846 Mission Gorge Rd in Santee, CA. We answer the phone 24 hours a day and send licensed, insured plumbers to homes and businesses in Santee, El Cajon and nearby East County communities.

You call, the dispatcher asks what is happening and where, and we schedule the next available licensed plumber. Same-day visits are available when the schedule allows. You hear a clear price before work starts, and the plumber explains the options if more than one fix exists.

How we think about the work

A good repair solves the cause, not just the symptom. A drain cleared once and blocked again a month later has a cause the plumber missed, such as roots, a belly in the pipe or a worn joint. We would rather tell you that on the first visit than clear the same clog twice.

When a job needs a permit, the plumber tells you before work starts. The City of Santee exempts simple repairs such as stopping leaks and clearing blockages from a building permit when no valves, pipes or fixtures are replaced or rearranged. Larger work, such as replacing concealed pipe, is treated as new work. The city's Planning and Building staff can confirm what applies to your address.

Homes and businesses we serve

Most calls come from single-family homes, but we also handle shops, restaurants and offices along Mission Gorge Road and in the surrounding commercial areas. Business calls often involve shared drain lines, grease and backflow devices, which are covered on our commercial plumbing page.

Rental owners and property managers can call the same line. Tell the dispatcher who holds the keys and who approves the work, so the plumber is not stuck at the door while a tenant waits.

How plumbers are licensed in California

California licenses plumbing contractors through the Contractors State License Board, under the C-36 plumbing classification. The board lets you look up a contractor by license number or business name. Plumbing work itself follows the California Plumbing Code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code published by IAPMO.

Those are public standards, not claims about us. Use them as a checklist when you compare any plumber, and ask to see the license before work starts.

Plain language about limits

A plumber stops the water, repairs the plumbing and tests the result. We do not repair drywall, flooring or mold, and we say so up front. If a leak has soaked a wall or a floor, call your insurer, take photos and ask the plumber for a short note on the cause.

We also do not guess. If a problem needs a camera, a pressure test or a second look, the plumber says so and explains why before moving ahead.
