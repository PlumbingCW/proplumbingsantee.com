What plumbing installation covers

Plumbing installation is planned work: setting new fixtures, adding a bathroom or a kitchen line, running new supply and drain pipes and installing devices that protect your water. It differs from drain cleaning and other repair work because the goal is a new system, not fixing a failed one.

We work from the California Plumbing Code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code, and follow the City of Santee's permit process where it applies.

Santee permits and inspections

The City of Santee exempts some small repairs from a building permit, such as stopping leaks and clearing blockages, but says replacing or rearranging valves, pipes or fixtures is a different matter, and replacing a failed concealed pipe needs a permit. New installations that add or move pipes generally fall on the permit side.

Ask the Planning and Building department about your project. They can tell you which permit applies and whether an inspection follows.

Planning a remodel with the plumber early

The best time to involve a plumber in a remodel is before the walls are drawn. Moving a toilet or a shower changes drain slope, vent paths and sometimes structural framing, and a layout that looks good on paper may not drain properly. A short planning visit saves tearing out finished work later.

Bring plans, photos or a sketch. The plumber checks where the existing lines run, whether the slab or the floor framing allows new drains and what the code needs for venting and cleanouts. If your project involves new gas lines, a new water heater or a repipe, the work is easier to schedule as one package.

Choosing fixtures that last

Fixtures vary widely in quality. A well-made faucet has a solid brass body, a ceramic cartridge and a finish that resists mineral spotting. A good toilet has a full-size trapway, a reliable fill valve and a flapper that seals cleanly. Look for fixtures with readily available parts, since a repair is cheaper when parts are easy to find.

In hard water areas such as Santee, a finish that resists scale and a faucet with a removable aerator pay off. Padre Dam Municipal Water District's 2023 report lists hardness of roughly 9 to 18 grains per gallon depending on the plant, so expect to descale aerators and showerheads from time to time.

Code and permit basics for new plumbing

New fixtures and new lines are installed under the California Plumbing Code, based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC). It covers pipe sizing, venting, trap arms, cleanout access and backflow protection. Plans for bigger jobs may need a permit and an inspection.

The City of Santee exempts simple repairs such as stopping leaks and clearing blockages when no valves, pipes or fixtures are replaced or rearranged. Moving or adding fixtures is a different category, so confirm with the city before you start.
