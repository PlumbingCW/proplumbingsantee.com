Burst pipe: what to do in the first five minutes

A burst pipe is the most urgent call a plumber gets. Shut off the main water valve first, then call our 24/7 line. We schedule same-day visits when a technician is free, and the dispatcher can stay on the phone while you work through the steps below.

Close the main valve

Turn it clockwise until it stops. Know where it is before an emergency happens.

Open a faucet low in the house

Draining remaining water relieves pressure and reduces further leakage.

Switch off power near water

Only if you can reach the panel dry. Never touch an outlet in standing water.

Move belongings and take photos

Lift items off wet floors and record the damage for your insurer.

What usually causes a pipe to burst

Pipes fail from age and corrosion, from high water pressure, from physical damage during renovation or digging, and from ground movement. In Santee, the Padre Dam hazard plan notes that earthquakes on faults such as the San Andreas and Elsinore could disrupt the district's imported supply and stress pipelines, which is a reminder to know your own shutoff.

High static pressure can break a weak joint. A pressure regulator and a gauge on a hose bib let you check yours. Elevation changes pressure, so hillside areas such as Sky Ranch are worth a reading.

Permits and emergency work in Santee

The City of Santee's building page says replacing concealed water, drain or waste pipe that fails is new work that needs a permit. It also says that emergency equipment replacements must file the application within the next two working business days. Ask the Planning and Building department how that applies to your repair.

Water damage restoration and insurance claims are separate from the plumbing repair. We fix the pipe and give you written notes of the work, which you can pass to your insurer.

Source: City of Santee, Work Not Requiring a Building Permit.

Code rules behind a pipe repair

Repairs in California follow the California Plumbing Code, which is built on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC) published by IAPMO. For a burst pipe that means approved pipe and fittings, supports at the right spacing and a pressure test before the wall is closed.

Ask the plumber what material is going in and whether the repair is one section or a longer run. A short section can be spliced. A pipe that has failed in several places is a sign to plan a larger fix.
