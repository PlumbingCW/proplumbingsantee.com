What we verified about East Elliott

Real estate listing sites use the name East Elliott for an area of Santee that includes the Carlton Oaks and Weston communities. It is a listing-site grouping and the city does not publish a boundary for it.

Unexplained wet spots

A damp wall or a spinning meter calls for leak detection before any wall is opened.

Pools, patios and outdoor plumbing

Houses with pools and patios often add outdoor hose bibs, gas lines for grills and irrigation tied into the main supply. Each tie-in adds a place for a leak or a backflow risk.

Check outdoor bibs for drips each spring and make sure any irrigation connection has a working backflow preventer. A plumber can inspect them during any visit.

Water heater habits that cost nothing

Set the thermostat near 120 degrees Fahrenheit. It lowers scald risk and slows scale. Look at the discharge pipe from the temperature and pressure relief valve to make sure it is open and not capped, and check the floor under the tank for moisture every few months.

If you smell gas near the heater, leave the house first and call your gas utility from outside. Do not switch lights or appliances on or off on the way out.

Water and sewer service

Your water bill names the utility for your address, and Padre Dam publishes a yearly report on what is in the supply, including hardness. Read the figure for your treatment plant before deciding whether a softener is worth it.

Mineral scale coats heating elements and shortens tank life. An annual tank flush is a simple habit, and a softener or filter cuts the load.
