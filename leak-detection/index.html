When a hidden leak becomes urgent

A leak behind a wall or under a floor damages drywall, framing and flooring the longer it runs. If you hear water with every tap off, see a wet spot spreading or smell mildew, call the 24/7 line. We book same-day visits when a technician is free, and the dispatcher can talk you through the first steps.

Shut off the main water valve if the leak is active and visible. Then switch off power to any wet area at the panel, only if you can reach the panel without stepping in water.

Signs of a leak you cannot see

Hidden leaks give hints long before they show water.

  • ✓
    Meter keeps movingTurn off every fixture and appliance, then watch the meter. A moving dial or a flowing leak indicator means water is escaping somewhere.
  • ✓
    Unexplained high billA bill that jumps with no change in habits is the most common first sign.
  • ✓
    Warm spot on the floorHot water lines under a slab can warm the surface above them.
  • ✓

  • ✓
    Stains and musty smellBrown rings on a ceiling or a damp odor in a closet point to moisture inside the wall.

How a leak is found without opening everything

Method matters. Each tool narrows the search and saves demolition.

  1. 1

    Test the system

    Pressure and meter checks tell us whether the leak is on the supply side, inside the home or in a fixture.

  2. 2

    Listen

    Acoustic ground and wall microphones pick up the sound of escaping water through concrete and drywall.

  3. 3

    Look for heat or moisture

    A thermal camera and a moisture meter show temperature and wetness patterns behind surfaces.

  4. 4

    Confirm before cutting

    Two methods agreeing on the same spot justify opening a small access hole.

Hard water, high bills and your meter

Padre Dam Municipal Water District supplies Santee, and its 2023 water quality report lists hardness of roughly 9 to 18 grains per gallon depending on the treatment plant. Scale from hard water can foul shutoff valves, toilet fill valves and water heater fittings, and those are the places a slow leak often starts.

If your bill rises, read the meter at the curb, wait an hour with all water off and read it again. Any change means a leak, and that is the time to call before the next bill arrives.

Source: Padre Dam Municipal Water District, 2023 Water Quality Report.

Guidelines the plumber follows to confirm a leak

A leak call starts with the water meter. The plumber closes all fixtures and reads the leak indicator, then isolates sections with shutoff valves. Acoustic and thermal tools narrow the location, and a pressure test confirms it before any wall or floor is opened.

Repairs that follow detection are made under the California Plumbing Code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC). Keep the detection notes and photos, since insurers often ask for them.
