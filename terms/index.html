Using this site

This website gives general plumbing information for homeowners in Santee, California. It is not a substitute for an on-site inspection. Every home is different, and a licensed plumber should assess your situation.

Services and pricing

Services are booked by phone. You receive a clear price before work starts, and any change to the scope is discussed with you first. Prices depend on the problem, access and time of the call.

Call recording

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes.

Safety

In an emergency involving gas, fire or injury, call 911 first. For a gas smell, leave the building and call 911 or your gas utility before calling us.

Accuracy of information

We work to keep the information on this site accurate. Local rules, permit requirements and water district details can change, so confirm them with the City of Santee or your water district.

Limits

We stop the water and repair the plumbing. Drywall, flooring and water damage cleanup are separate trades.
