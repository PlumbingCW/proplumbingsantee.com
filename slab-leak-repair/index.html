Why a slab leak is an emergency

Water under a slab washes out soil, raises the water bill and can stain flooring from below. Call the 24/7 line if you hear water under the floor, feel warm tile, or the meter spins with everything off. Same-day visits are booked when available.

Close the main water valve to stop the loss, then use the hot water sparingly or not at all until a plumber confirms which line is leaking. If the water heater is on a slab line, switch it to vacation mode so it does not run dry.

Symptoms of a slab leak

Slab leaks usually announce themselves in a few ways.

  • ✓
    A warm or hot floor spotA leak in a hot line warms the concrete above it.
  • ✓
    Hissing under the floorQuiet rooms at night make the sound easy to hear.
  • ✓
    Meter runs with taps offThe classic sign of any hidden leak.
  • ✓
    Cracks or damp flooringWater pushing up through the slab can swell wood floors and lift carpet.
  • ✓
    Low hot water pressureA leak in the hot line steals water before it reaches fixtures.

Why slabs leak in older Santee homes

Many Southern California homes were built on concrete slabs, with water lines running through or under the foundation. Santee added a lot of housing between 1958 and 1980, so supply lines under slabs may now be fifty years old or older. Over decades, copper can develop pinholes, and the cause varies by pipe, soil and water chemistry.

Padre Dam Municipal Water District disinfects with chloramine and reports hardness of roughly 9 to 18 grains per gallon depending on the plant. A plumber does not blame the water for a leak, but a pattern of pinholes in one home is worth noting to the plumber.

Three ways to fix a slab leak

The right path depends on the pipe, the number of leaks and the floor finish.

OptionHow it worksBest when
Spot repairCut the slab above the leak and replace the damaged sectionOne leak, sound pipe elsewhere
RerouteAbandon the slab line and run a new line through walls or atticSeveral leaks, or a hard-to-reach line
RepipeReplace the supply system, see repipingWidespread pinholes and failing pipe

Standards the plumber works to

Slab leak repairs follow the California Plumbing Code, based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC), with a pressure test to confirm the repair holds. Materials that carry drinking water should be certified to NSF/ANSI 61.

Detection comes first. Acoustic and thermal tools find the spot, and the plumber explains the options before any concrete is cut.

What to do if you suspect a slab leak

Act early to limit damage.

  • ✓
    Check the water meterWith every fixture off, a moving dial means water is escaping.
  • ✓
    Shut off the main valveClosing it stops the loss while you wait.
  • ✓
    Turn off the water heaterThis keeps the tank from running dry.
  • ✓
    Move belongings off damp floorsRugs, boxes and furniture should come up first.
