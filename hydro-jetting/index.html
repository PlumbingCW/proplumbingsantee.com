When a backed-up line needs jetting right away

A line that backs up the second time in a month is an emergency in slow motion. The clog keeps coming back because a cable opens a channel but leaves the coating on the pipe walls. Call the 24/7 line and we can schedule jetting the same day when a visit is available.

Until the jetter arrives, limit water use. Do not run the dishwasher or washer, and keep the lowest fixtures in the house clear of belongings. If sewage is coming up in the tub or a floor drain, keep children and pets out of that room.

What hydro jetting does

Hydro jetting uses a hose with a nozzle that fires water at high pressure against the inside of the pipe. The jets face backward and forward, so the nozzle pulls itself along while stripping grease, scale and soft root growth off the walls. The debris flushes out with the water instead of being pushed along.

It is a cleaning method, not a repair. A jetter will not seal a cracked pipe, and on a badly damaged line it can make a weak spot worse. That is why a camera check often comes first or right after, and why sewer inspection pairs well with jetting.

Lines where jetting earns its place

Jetting costs more than a snake, so it should solve a problem a snake cannot.

  • ✓
    Restaurant and kitchen linesFat builds up in layers that a cable only pierces. Jetting removes the layers. See commercial plumbing for food-service sites.
  • ✓
    Lines that clog againIf the same drain needs a cable every few weeks, the walls are coated and jetting resets the pipe.
  • ✓
    Roots that regrowJetting clears fine root hairs. Thick roots need a cutter first.
  • ✓
    Older cast ironRough, scaled interiors catch paper. Scouring smooths the path, if the pipe is sound.

Santee conditions that affect jetting

Padre Dam Municipal Water District maintains about 169 miles of wastewater mains, and the private lateral that ties your house into one of them is yours to maintain. Lateral materials vary by decade, with clay, cast iron and plastic all turning up in East County homes. A technician should confirm the pipe material and condition before turning the pressure up.

Wastewater from Santee flows to Padre Dam Municipal Water District's influent pump station, so the main in the street is the district's responsibility. Jetting is for your private line from the house to that connection. If a jetted line still backs up and neighbors report the same, call the district.

Source: Padre Dam Municipal Water District hazard mitigation annex (San Diego County, 2023).

Before the jetting crew arrives

Small steps now make the work safer and faster.

Locate the cleanout

The jet hose usually enters through an outdoor cleanout. Clear plants and storage from around it.

Stop the flow

Hold laundry and dishwasher runs. Extra water adds to the backup.

Move valuables

If a floor drain is overflowing, lift anything stored on the garage or laundry floor.

Keep access clear

Gate locked? Leave a note or meet the plumber at the side yard.
