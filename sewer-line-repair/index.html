When a damaged sewer line cannot wait

A broken sewer line leaks sewage into the soil and lets roots in, so it will not heal on its own. When the backup returns after every cleaning or the yard stays wet near the cleanout, call the 24/7 line. We schedule same-day visits when a technician is available.

In the meantime, limit water use in the house and avoid flushing. Keep children and pets away from any soggy patch in the yard, and do not dig near the line yourself.

Signs the line has failed, not just clogged

A clog is removable. A failed pipe is not. These signs point to damage.

  • ✓
    Backups return within weeksThe blockage keeps rebuilding at the same spot, which is typical of a crack or a low section that holds water.
  • ✓
    Wet or sunken groundA soggy patch or small dip along the line suggests leakage or a collapse.
  • ✓
    Sewer odor outsideA smell near the foundation or cleanout can mean a leak underground.
  • ✓
    Cable brings up soil or fragmentsSand, clay chips or pieces of pipe on the cable show the wall is breaking down.
  • ✓
    Cable stops shortIf the machine cannot pass a certain distance, something is blocking or broken there.

Three ways to repair a sewer line

The camera decides which one fits. Each method has a different disruption and lifespan profile.

MethodWhat it isBest when
Spot repairDig down and replace a short sectionDamage is local and the rest of the pipe is sound
Pipe liningA resin liner cured inside the old pipeThe run is long, mostly intact and access is tight
Full replacementNew pipe laid in a trenchThe pipe is collapsed, bellied or crushed along much of its length

A short camera pass first prevents paying for the wrong method. See sewer inspection for how the scope works.

Santee ground and pipe conditions

Santee grew from under 2,000 people in 1950 to 25,750 in 1970, and a good share of the housing was built in that window. Lines installed then may now be fifty years old or more, so a camera look before any quote saves a lot of guessing.

The line from your house to the street connection is private. Padre Dam Municipal Water District handles the wastewater main beyond that point. If the failure is past the connection, the plumber will say so and point you to the district.

What happens on a sewer repair job

The sequence keeps surprises low.

  1. 1

    Scope the line

    A camera locates the break, the belly or the root intrusion and records its depth.

  2. 2

    Pick the method

    The plumber explains spot repair, lining and replacement and which one the footage supports.

  3. 3

    Confirm permits

    Santee's building page says replacing a failed concealed drain or sewer pipe is new work that needs a permit. We follow the city's requirement for the job.

  4. 4

    Repair and re-test

    After the work, the line is cabled or run with water and the camera is re-run to confirm.

Permits and code for sewer repairs

Sewer line work follows the California Plumbing Code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC). It covers pipe material, slope, bedding, cleanout access and testing. Digging in the street or a public right of way brings its own rules and usually a permit.

The City of Santee treats a failed concealed pipe that is replaced as new work needing a permit, so ask Planning and Building what applies to your address before the trench is opened.

What to do while sewage is backing up

Protect people and property until the plumber arrives.

  • ✓
    Stop using waterNo flushing, showers, dishwasher or laundry.
  • ✓
    Keep people and pets awayRaw sewage carries bacteria. Keep children out of the area.
  • ✓
    Shut off the main if water is flowingOverflow from a fixture may mean a blocked main.
  • ✓
    Take photos and notesRecord the time and what you saw, for your insurer.
