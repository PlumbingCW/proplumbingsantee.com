Carlton Hills facts we could verify

Carlton Hills is a residential neighborhood in Santee. Home listing sites place its addresses in ZIP code 92071, around Mast Boulevard, Carlton Hills Boulevard and Carlton Oaks Drive, with Santee Lakes and Town Center close by.

The City of Santee history page says a development firm, the Carlton Company, bought 4,300 acres in 1958, and that Mast Boulevard is named for Bill Mast, the company's early president. The city does not publish a build year or a formal boundary for Carlton Hills, so we do not state one here.

Listing sites show a mix of single-story, two-story and split-level houses, plus townhomes and condos. A mixed neighborhood has no single pipe type, which is why the guidance below is about checking your own house.

Sources: City of Santee history page, public home listing sites. Boundaries are not formally published.

Find out what is actually in your walls

Pipe material depends on the house, not the neighborhood name. Copper is brown or green and takes a magnet to nothing. Galvanized steel is gray, attracts a magnet and often shows rust at joints. PEX is flexible plastic in red, blue or white. Drain lines may be plastic, cast iron or clay.

A plumber can confirm material and condition during a visit. For drains, a camera look shows the real state of the line before anyone recommends a repair. See sewer inspection for what that visit covers.

Water quality near Santee Lakes and Town Center

Padre Dam Municipal Water District provides water to Santee. Its 2023 Water Quality Report lists hardness of roughly 9 to 18 grains per gallon depending on the treatment plant, and chloramine as the disinfectant. Your bill shows which source supplies your address.

The USGS rates water above about 10.5 grains per gallon as very hard. Hard water leaves scale on heating elements and fixtures, so a tank water heater may need flushing sooner. See water softener and filtration if scale shows up in your kettle or showerhead.
