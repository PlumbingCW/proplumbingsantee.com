Inside Santee

These places are inside the city, and each one shapes the kind of plumbing calls we get.

Carlton Hills

A residential neighborhood in Santee. Homes built in the decades after the Carlton Company bought land here in 1958 may still have original supply and drain lines, so check them before a remodel.

Sky Ranch

A hillside community on Rattlesnake Mountain, the city's highest point at 1,198 feet. Pressure differs by elevation, so a gauge on a hose bib is a cheap check.

Mission Gorge Road

The main commercial corridor, with centers such as Santee Trolley Square and Mission Gorge Square. Shared drain lines make commercial plumbing calls common.

Santee Lakes, Mast Park and Carlton Oaks

Recreation areas that draw weekend crowds. Weekend plumbing calls are normal for us, and the line stays open.

Neighborhood pages inside Santee

Neighboring communities

Lakeside sits to the east of Santee. La Mesa and Spring Valley lie to the south and southwest, near El Cajon. Water districts differ across the area. Padre Dam Municipal Water District serves Santee and also lists Lakeside and El Cajon in its service area, and Helix Water District delivers water to La Mesa and El Cajon.

Your water bill names your district, including in Spring Valley. Each district publishes an annual water quality report, which is the place to look for hardness. See water softener and filtration if scale is a concern.

How dispatch works across the area

One phone number covers every town on this page. The dispatcher asks for your address, the problem and whether water is running, then sends the next available plumber. Travel time inside Santee and to El Cajon is short, and the dispatcher tells you what to expect when you call.

Nights, weekends and holidays follow the same process. If the problem is an active leak, the dispatcher can walk you through the main shutoff while the plumber is on the way, so water damage stays small.

Water and sewer service differs by address

Padre Dam Municipal Water District provides water and wastewater service to Santee, and its service area also includes El Cajon, Lakeside and other East County communities. Helix Water District delivers water to La Mesa, Lemon Grove and El Cajon. The utility on your bill is the one to contact about the water meter, the street main or a sewer main backup.

The line between the meter and your house belongs to you, and so does the sewer lateral that runs from the house to the main. Work on both lines follows the California Plumbing Code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC). A plumber can tell you where your responsibility ends. See water service line repair and sewer line repair for what those jobs involve.
